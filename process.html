How it works

From idea to live in 48 hours.

No drawn-out timelines, no confusing tech-speak, no meetings that go nowhere. Four simple steps, and your business is online by the end of the week.

4
simple steps
48h
from go-ahead to live
0
confusing jargon
1
Hour 0 · You

You reach out

Send us a short email about your business — what you do, who you serve, and what you'd love your website to do for you. That's the whole "form." Two minutes, tops.

2
Hour 1–4 · Together

We plan it together

We reply fast with a couple of friendly questions and an exact quote. You send over your logo, photos, and any words you already have. We agree on the plan — in plain English.

3
Day 1–2 · Us

We design & build

Your custom site comes to life. We'll share a preview link so you can see it taking shape and tell us anything you'd like changed — no surprises at the finish line.

4
By hour 48 · You're live

We launch it

You give the thumbs-up, we connect your web address and publish. We double-check everything works on phones and computers, then hand you the keys. You're officially online.

Your part is easy

All we need from you to begin.

Don't have everything ready? That's fine — we'll help you gather it, and we can even help write the words.

Start the 48 hours
  • Your business name & what you do
  • A logo & a few photos (or we'll suggest options)
  • Your hours, location & contact details
  • A rough idea of the pages you want
  • A site or two you like the look of (optional)
The 48-hour promise

Fast doesn't mean rushed.

We can move quickly because we keep things focused — one project, one clear plan, real attention.

The clock starts when you're ready

The 48 hours begins the moment you give us the go-ahead and the basics — not before. No pressure while you decide.

You see it before it's final

A preview link lets you watch your site come together and request tweaks — nothing goes live until you love it.

We stay reachable after

Need a small change next month? Just email. We don't disappear once the site is live — we're a quick message away.

Ready when you are.

Send the first email today and your site could be live by the day after tomorrow.
