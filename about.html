Our story

We're students at SMU — and we build websites to help.

aigrowthly started at Southern Methodist University in Dallas with one simple frustration: great local businesses were being quoted thousands of dollars for a basic website. We knew we could do better — and do it fairly.

Why we started

It didn't sit right with us.

We kept hearing the same story from owners we knew — a quote for $3,000, $5,000, even more, for a website that was really just a swapped-out template. For a brand-new shop or a one-person trade, that's the kind of money that decides whether you open at all.

So we put our skills to work. We're studying, building, and genuinely good at this — and we'd rather use that to help fifty local owners get online than overcharge a handful. Every site we build makes us sharper and grows our reputation. That's a deal we're happy with.

Fair price. Real craft. People over profit.
